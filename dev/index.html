Dine with us

A kosher kitchen with a Mexicali heart, and a bar that takes its agave seriously.

Lenny's Casita started as a takeout-only ghost kitchen with one stubborn idea: soulful Mexicali flavor belongs in the kosher world. It's now a full-service restaurant and bar on Pico Boulevard.

Chef Lenny cooks from California roots and fine-dining training — tacos, flatbreads and plates that are bright, craveable and his own. Behind the bar, cocktails are built on fresh, natural ingredients, with tequila and mezcal at center stage.

Mi casa es su casa

From a borrowed kitchen to Pico Boulevard.

  1. Before the Casita

    The secret special

    Hired to open Charcoal with an Israeli-inspired menu, Lenny slipped Lamb Shawarma Tacos onto the soft opening as an off-menu special. They sold out every night.

  2. March 15, 2020

    The dining rooms close

    California shuts restaurant dining. Lenny's former meat supplier offers kitchen space during off-hours — enough to start cooking again.

  3. May 11, 2020

    A ghost kitchen opens

    Lenny's Casita launches for takeout and delivery only. Within a week, the whole team is rehired — Cesar Mateo, now behind the bar, joins the family in these ghost-kitchen days.

  4. Today

    A real casita

    A full-service restaurant and bar at 8823 W Pico Blvd, still cooking to make every guest feel at home.

After dark · Cocktails by Cesar Mateo

Tequila & and mezcal take center stage.

Cesar Mateo came to LA from Oaxaca at 11 and worked his way from dishwasher to bar manager at Guerrilla Tacos before building this list for Lenny's. No pre-made syrups, no artificial flavors: fresh-squeezed lime and house-made bitters, salts and spice blends.

  1. 01 / 06

    Spicy Margarita Balagán

    Spicy · Earthy · Refreshing

    Cesar's favorite high-school snack — cucumber, lime and Tajín — turned into a drink: jalapeño-infused tequila, cucumber and pineapple juice, agave, and house-made Tajín on the rim.

    $21Pairs with quesabirria tacos

  2. 02 / 06

    Waterpamelon

    Refreshing · Sweet · Citrusy

    A paloma on a watermelon base, sweetened with oleo saccharum — lemon zest drawn out over three days — with house lime salt and a grapefruit soda finish.

    $21Pairs with birria ramen

  3. 03 / 06

    616 Gimlet

    Floral · Botanical · Fruity

    Gin and floral lychee with a butterfly pea flower infusion that turns from blue to purple when the citrus goes in, finished with an orchid.

    $20Pairs with Casita cauliflower

  4. 04 / 06

    Carefuleta

    Bright · Citrusy · Licorice

    A Tel Aviv beach day in a glass: frozen limonana spiked with arak, blended with coconut, mint and lime zest.

    $21Pairs with arayis or the brisket pita

  5. 05 / 06

    Michelada de Casita

    Tangy · Spicy · Refreshing

    Lenny's own go-to. A house “clamato” of roasted heirloom tomato, lime and spice, topped with Lenny's Casita Mexican Lager, brewed with Long Beach Beer Lab.

    $14Pairs with sizzling fajitas

  6. 06 / 06

    La Manzana Whiskey Sour

    Decadent · Velvety · Tart

    Their take on a New York sour — the Big Apple, la manzana. Banana-infused whiskey, silan, house cocoa bitters and a red wine float, delivered in a treasure chest of smoke.

    $24Pairs with the gaucho bone-in ribeye
